Episode 01: A Little Confession

i don't really know where to start, but i think i should finally say this.

at first, i only thought u were pretty. it was just a simple crush, something i thought would pass eventually.

then i started talking to u. and somehow, that little crush became something more.

the more i got to know u, the more i found myself thinking about u, waiting for ur messages, and wanting to know u a little deeper.

i don't know exactly when it happened, but somewhere between all our conversations, i stopped simply liking u.

i fell for u :)

i thought i could keep this feeling simple, but i guess that's where i lost.

u became someone i genuinely care about, someone i miss, someone i want to keep close.

so i think it's time i stop calling this just a crush.

im into u, bibeng.

so... bibeng, can i be ur bf??

fyi, im always ready to be ur man.
